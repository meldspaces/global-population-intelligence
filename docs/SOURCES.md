# Data Sources Registry

| Source | URL | Topics | Years | Geo | Frequency | Notes |
|--------|-----|--------|-------|-----|-----------|-------|
| Our World in Data | https://ourworldindata.org | Homicide, conflict, diet, health, GDP | 1950–2026 | Country | Annual | Chart Data API |
| World Bank WDI | https://data.worldbank.org | Economy, demographics, climate proxies | 1960–2026 | Country | Annual | Free API |
| UNODC | https://data.unodc.org | Homicide, drug use (stimulants, opioids, etc.) | ~1990–2026 | Country + some cities | Annual | Prevalence by substance |
| WHO / IHME GBD | https://www.healthdata.org | Mortality, mental illness, substance | 1990–2023+ | Country | Annual | High-quality estimates |
| UCDP / PRIO | https://ucdp.uu.se | Armed conflict, battle deaths | 1946–2026 | Country / Conflict | Annual | Precise conflict names |
| FAO | https://www.fao.org/faostat | Diet composition, food supply | 1961–2026 | Country | Annual | Macronutrients |
| Historical | Academic / League of Nations reconstructions | Pre-1950 war deaths, crime | 1930–1950 | Country | Static | Estimates clearly flagged |

**Estimates:** Explicitly marked. Methods (interpolation, regional averages, model-based) documented per series.
